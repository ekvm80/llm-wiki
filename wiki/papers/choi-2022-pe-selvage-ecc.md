## Summary

To maximize the uniaxial direct-tension strain-hardening performance and crack self-healing efficiency of eco-friendly cement-free alkali-activated slag (AAS: GGBS 90 % + powdered slaked lime Ca(OH)₂ 10 wt %, w/b = 0.30) composites, the team of Dr. Jeong-Il Choi, researcher H. H. Nguyễn, and Prof. Bang-Yeon Lee at Chonnam National University precisely characterized the early cracking behavior, microcrack width ($w_c$), post-healing reloading tensile strength ($f_{t-h}$), and tensile strain capacity ($\epsilon_h$) of three composites combining hydrophobic ultra-high-molecular-weight polyethylene (PE: length 18 mm, diameter 12 µm, $f_t$ = 2700 MPa, E = 88 GPa) fibers with hydrophilic polyvinyl alcohol (PVA: length 8 mm, diameter 40 µm, $f_t$ = 1560 MPa, E = 41 GPa) fibers in this 2022 *Construction and Building Materials* paper. The PE 1.5 % + PVA 0.25 % hybrid composite (E1.5-V0.25) controlled its initial crack width to **66.20 µm** through the nucleation effect of the hydrophilic PVA fibers and, after 28 days of water-immersion healing, achieved a tensile strength of **8.51 MPa** and a total tensile strain capacity of **8.27 %**, demonstrating outstanding self-healing recovery performance.

## Key Contributions

- **Development of a high-ductility self-healing AAS composite based on PE–PVA organic fiber hybridization**: synergistic combination of the high tensile-strain-capacity contribution of hydrophobic PE fibers with the calcium-leaching acceleration mechanism of hydrophilic PVA fibers.
- **Quantitative control of microcrack width ($w_c$) and crack count upon cracking**: the hybrid E1.5-V0.25 increased the initial number of cracks to 36.30 compared with the single-PE E1.5-V0 (30.00), while reducing the average crack width from 80.10 µm to 66.20 µm.
- **Demonstrated recovery of more than 100 % of tensile strength and ductility after self-healing**: the healed E1.5-V0.25 specimen exhibited a tensile strength of 8.51 MPa and a tensile strain capacity of 8.27 %, showing more-than-complete mechanical recovery relative to the pre-healed initial state.
- **Identification of the self-healing products via EDS and SEM**: proved that high-purity calcite ($\mathrm{CaCO_3}$) crystals with a Ca/Si ratio of 250 or higher formed around the PVA fibers and completely sealed the crack gaps.

## Methods

- Binder mix: GGBS ([unlabeled oxide] 17.5 %, CaO 56.8 %, Al₂O₃ 8.5 %) 90 % + Ca(OH)₂ 10 %, w/b = 0.30, silica sand (s/b = 0.40), SP 0.4–0.6 %, VMA 0.01–0.05 %, antifoaming agent 0.1 %.
- Fiber combinations:
  - **E1.5-V0**: PE fiber 1.5 vol % (alone)
  - **E1.5-V0.25 (hybrid)**: PE fiber 1.5 vol % + PVA fiber 0.25 vol %
  - **E1.75-V0**: PE fiber 1.75 vol % (alone)
- Testing: dogbone direct-tension preloading (unloaded upon reaching 0.5 % tensile strain) $\rightarrow$ 28 days of water-immersion self-healing $\rightarrow$ reloading tensile test and optical-microscopy analysis of the crack closure ratio.
